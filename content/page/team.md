The team is me. The team is you, too. We encourage [pull requests](https://dbatools.io/firstpull) and [development participation](https://dbatools.io/contributing). There's also a [#dbatools channel](https://dbatools.io/slack) on the [SQL Server Community Slack](https://dbatools.io/slack) if you'd like to discuss the module or just hang out.

Hey, I'm Chrissy, the original creator and current maintainer of dbatools. I'm a [Cajun](http://www.realcajunrecipes.com) living in Belgium and a SQL Server DBA with over 20 years of experience.

I've been a fan of Linux and Open Source since I was first introduced back in the 90's, right around the time I moved to California to work in tech. I've loved SQL Server for nearly as long. And of course, PowerShell, which I've worked with since 2005.
