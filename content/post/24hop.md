Constantine and I recently presented for 24 Hours of PASS and the video is now online! Our goal in this intro-level session was to help make PowerShell feel approachable and demonstrate its immediate value with commands like [Get-DbaLastGoodCheckDb](https://dbatools.io/Get-DbaLastGoodCheckDb), [Test-DbaSpn](https://dbatools.io/Test-DbaSpn) and [Invoke-DbaDiagnosticQuery](https://dbatools.io/Invoke-DbaDiagnosticQuery).
